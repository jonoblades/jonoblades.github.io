Wordley

Wordley is a word puzzle game where players must guess a hidden word within a limited number of attempts.

It challenges players' vocabulary and deduction skills, providing a fun and engaging way to improve language proficiency.

The game is inspired by popular word puzzle games and aims to offer a unique twist with its own set of rules and challenges.

Sudoku

Sudoku is a classic number puzzle game where players must fill a 9x9 grid with digits so that each column, each row, and each of the nine 3x3 subgrids contain all of the digits from 1 to 9.

It challenges players' logical thinking and problem-solving skills, providing a stimulating and rewarding experience for puzzle enthusiasts.

Sudoku is widely regarded as a game that enhances concentration and memory, making it both an entertaining and intellectually rewarding pastime.

Futile

Futile is a turn-based tile game for 2-4 players. The aim is to be the first player to empty your hand by building valid sets and runs from your tiles.

Each player starts with 10 tiles and draws one at the beginning of every turn. You can then play a set of three or more tiles with the same number in different colours, or a run of three or more consecutive numbers in the same colour. If you cannot, or choose not to, play a meld, pass a tile to the next player.

The game ends when a player plays their last tile. Scores are based on the value of the tiles in each player's melds, minus the tiles left in their hand, with the player who goes out earning an additional 10 points. Good play means watching the draw pile and using your high-value tiles before they become a burden.
