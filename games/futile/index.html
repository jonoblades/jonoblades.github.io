How to play

Futile is a turn-based tile game for 2-4 players. The objective is to be the first to play all your tiles by forming valid sets and runs.

Setup

  1. Each player starts with 10 tiles.
  2. The remaining tiles form the draw pile.

Gameplay

  1. On your turn, you start by drawing a tile from the draw pile. You then:
    1. Play a valid set or run from your hand onto the table.
    2. Pass your turn by selecting a tile to pass to the next player.
  2. A set consists of 3 or more tiles of the same number but different colours.
  3. A run consists of 3 or more consecutive numbers of the same colour.
  4. The first player to play all their tiles ends the game, and scores an additional 10 points.
  5. The winner is determined by the player with the highest score at the end of the game.
  6. Scoring is based on the tiles each player has in their melds, minus any tiles left in their hand at the end of the game.

Strategy Tips

  • Try to create sets and runs that use up your high-value tiles first.
  • Keep an eye on the draw pile and try to anticipate which tiles might come up next.
