--- title: Privacy Policy ---

Summary

I care about privacy, both my own and other people's, so I've tried to keep things simple.

Analytics

This site uses GoatCounter to give me a rough idea of which pages are being read and how people find the site.

GoatCounter is configured in a privacy-friendly way:

  • No cookies are stored on your device.
  • No advertising or marketing profiles are created.
  • I don't attempt to identify individual visitors.
  • I don't sell or share visitor data.

When you visit a page, the following information may be sent to GoatCounter:

  • The page being viewed.
  • The page you came from (if your browser provides a referrer).
  • Basic information about your browser and device.
  • An anonymised version of your IP address.
  • The date and time of the visit.

All of this information is anonymised and aggregated, and cannot be used to identify individual visitors.

I use this information only to understand things such as:

  • Which pages are popular.
  • Whether anyone is reading my writing.
  • Which websites or search engines are sending visitors here.

If you want to learn more about GoatCounter's privacy practices, you can visit their privacy page.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies for analytics or tracking purposes.

Your browser, extensions, or third-party services may still create their own cookies, but this website does not intentionally use any.

Links to Other Sites

Some pages contain links to other websites. If you follow one of those links, their privacy policies and practices apply, not mine.

Contact

If you contact me directly, any information you choose to send will be used only for the purpose of responding to you.

Changes

If I add new services or features that affect privacy, I'll update this page.
