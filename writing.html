Writing

I have started to write fiction, and have beendoing my best to improve my craft and explore new ideas. Trying not to pigeon-hole myself in a particular style or genre, I have instead been trying to connect a variety of stories and settings both thematically and by writing them all within the same world. Each of my stories contributes to a larger narrative tapestry about human connection.

Minding Bella

Short Story August 2026

A short story about Jess - a student whose routine is turned upside-down one Tuesday afternoon by a three-year old named Bella.

The story explores the themes of loneliness and the rewards of connection, despite the chaos it sometimes brings.

Charlie Tough: Travel Sickness

Novel In progress (2026)

A middle-grade science-fiction story about a boy whose tendency to become travel sick develops an unexpected connection to time travel.

This ongoing project began with a desire to write something fun that my kids would want to read. Combining my own love for sci-fi with my boys sense of adventure - and love of toilet jokes - I have set out to create a story that combines humour and imagination whilst grounding it with loss and emotional depth.

Fractured Echoes

Short Story In progress (2026)

Another short story, and still very much a work in progress, Fractured Echoes follows Mike Stevens, a former marine, as he tries to evade capture after escaping from 'The Facility'. Having received a headwound, combined with effects of drugs administered at The Facility, Mike struggles to distinguish reality from hallucination, leaving the reader wondering what is actually real.

The Optimiser

Short Story In progress (2026)

The Optimiser is a short story that follows Marcus Hale - an incredibly efficient businessman, whose only focus is to streamline processes and optimise efficiencies. However, this comes at a human cost as jobs are replaced with automated processes.

Originally an experiment to see how well AI can write, given a full prompt and direction, The Optimiser is currently being rewritten from scratch.

The Gracious Wall

Novel Concept (2026)

The Gracious Wall is a concept novel set in both the near future and a dystopian alternate reality, exploring themes of societal collapse, human resilience, and the importance of human connection.
