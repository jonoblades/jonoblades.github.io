Writing

I have recently started writing fiction and am working to improve my craft while exploring different ideas, styles and genres. Rather than limiting the stories to one type of setting, I am gradually connecting them through shared themes and a wider fictional world. Each story contributes in some way to a larger narrative about human connection.

Minding Bella

Short Story August 2026

A short story about Jess - a student whose routine is turned upside-down one Tuesday afternoon by a three-year-old named Bella.

The story explores loneliness and the unexpected rewards of human connection, even when that connection arrives with a certain amount of chaos.

Like most of her classmates, Jess loved Tuesdays. With an afternoon free of lectures and tutorials, Tuesday was probably her favourite day of the week. Unlike the other students, however, it wasn’t the lure of the various bars and clubs around the city that did it for her – in fact she hated all that – instead it was Bella.

Charlie Tough: Travel Sickness

Novel In progress (2026)

A middle-grade science-fiction story about a boy whose tendency to become travel sick develops an unexpected connection to time travel.

This ongoing project began with a desire to write something fun that my children would genuinely want to read. Combining my love of science fiction with my boys’ sense of adventure, and their appreciation for toilet jokes, I am creating a story that balances humour and imagination with loss, family and emotional depth.

I’m in a bit of a pickle now. Well, a bit more than a pickle – I’m in trouble; BIG trouble. In fact, I think I’ve gone and completely blown it. For me, for dad, for the whole world perhaps. But mostly for mum.

We moved into our new place two months ago. Well, technically it was two months ago for me, but I suppose for everyone else it’s in about… three and a half years. Oh man, this is going to get really complicated, super quickly! That’s the problem with time travel.

Fractured Echoes

Short Story In progress (2026)

Still very much a work in progress, Fractured Echoes follows Mike Stevens, a former Marine attempting to evade capture after escaping from a place known only as the Facility. A head wound, combined with the effects of drugs administered during his captivity, leaves Mike struggling to distinguish reality from hallucination. As his perceptions become less reliable, the reader is left to question how much of his experience is real.

Mike Stevens cursed as he pushed through the pain searing up his right thigh. He was grateful, of course, that he still had his leg; two inches, the camp medic had said, and it would have been a completely different story. But in some ways the result was the same – a medical discharge that exchanged what should have been a long and distinguished career in the special forces, to this incessant ache, one that quickly turned to agony with even the slightest amount of exertion.

The Optimiser

Short Story In progress (2026)

The Optimiser follows Marcus Hale, an exceptionally efficient businessman whose sole focus is streamlining processes and improving productivity. His success comes at a human cost as jobs and livelihoods are displaced by automation. The story originally began as an experiment in how effectively AI could write from a detailed prompt and creative direction. I am now rewriting it from scratch in my own voice.

Originally an experiment to see how well AI can write, given a full prompt and direction, The Optimiser is currently being rewritten from scratch.

The Gracious Wall

Novel Concept (2026)

The Gracious Wall is a concept novel set in both the near future and a dystopian alternate reality, exploring themes of societal collapse, human resilience, and the importance of human connection.
