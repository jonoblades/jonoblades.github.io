A short story about Kate - a student whose routine is turned upside-down one Tuesday afternoon by a three-year-old named Bella.

The story explores loneliness and the unexpected rewards of human connection, even when that connection arrives with a certain amount of chaos.

Like most of her classmates, Kate loved Tuesdays. With an afternoon free of lectures and tutorials, Tuesday was probably her favourite day of the week. Unlike the other students, however, it wasn’t the lure of the various bars and clubs around the city that did it for her – in fact she hated all that – instead it was Bella.
