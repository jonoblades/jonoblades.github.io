--- title: Wonder date: 2026-10-20 last_modified_at: 2026-10-20 word_count: 50 categories: - Non-fiction - Fifty Words tags: - Personal excerpt: > 50 words. ---

Hundreds of billions of galaxies floating in the vastness of space; stars and planets, too numerous to count, dancing in perfect synchronisation across a universe billions of light-years across. And I am here. Little me. Barely a breath in the grandness of space, yet unique and special and valued. Wonderful.

This piece was written for the 50 Word Non-Fiction competition by the Scottish Book Trust, as opposed to their regular monthly fiction competition.
