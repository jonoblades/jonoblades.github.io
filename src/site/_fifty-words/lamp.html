Shadows crawled up the cave walls and heckled Amara as the lamp oil burned low, its flickering flame slowly diminishing. The light had been her only companion, and as the dark monsters loomed above her, and wind moaned eerily down the dank tunnel, a silent tear fell down her cheek.
