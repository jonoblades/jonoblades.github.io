Not Mine

Sometimes I have a spark of creativity, but lack the attention span to write it myself. So I cheat and feed some carefully (and not so carefully) written prompts to AI, allowing it to generate the content for me.

I can't claim credit for something spat out by a machine, and I'm not sure I'd want to - most of it is slop. But perhaps it can serve as a source of inspiration or a starting point for future creative endeavors. Either way, I'm sharing it here for anyone who might find it interesting, amusing or even horrifying!

Waiting in the Quiet Queue

Poem AI written (September 2026)

Waiting in the Quiet Queue was written whilst waiting for a bot to transfer me to a member of my bank's chat support team. Having no feedback or indication that anything was happening, and having to occasionally 'do something' to prevent my session timing out, I started prompting AI to generate content based on my situation, which I then proceeded to post into the chat.

Eventually, after waiting over an hour an a half, and having no response from the human support team, I gave up, logged out and phoned them instead!
