Sailing

August 2026 - 50 words

Mike had always envied the kids who sailed little boats with their fathers. So, when he became a dad, he resolved to take Charlotte. Saturday morning came, and they carried their little white craft down to the water and watched it drift until the paper went soggy and it sank.

Raincoat

September 2026 - 50 words

“Don’t forget your raincoat Charlie,” called Mum as I ran out the door.

I glanced at the blue sky, rolled my eyes and shot down the path to catch up with Ellie.

I got back three hours later. Soaked to the core and wondering why I never listened to Mum.
